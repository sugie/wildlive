Architecture / Design Decisions

The full list — 19 numbered decisions — lives in docs/adr/0002-game-system-foundation.md. Highlights that future AI agents and reviewers must not miss:

  • Real wildlife only. No fictional or fantasy creatures. Mystery is expressed through rare real traits, not invented species.
  • Species ≠ Animal. Species is global master data; Animal is a captured individual with its own traits, sex, weight, rarity, and history. Two individuals of the same species are two distinct entities.
  • Design target: 120 Species — a goal, not a hard technical constant.
  • Hunter terminology is settled. "Explorer" is deprecated across all documents.
  • Guild-based Hunter pool. Top Hunters are a shared scarce resource across all players; basic-tier Hunters must always remain contractable so newcomers can always play. Player-to-player Hunter lending is not part of MVP.
  • Capture / no-capture outcome model. Weak-Hunter-in-hard-Region is not forbidden but often returns empty-handed.
  • Zoo is not a management sim. No food, land, staffing, cleaning, illness, or budget in MVP.
  • Zoo Value ranking, with diminishing returns baked in: mass-farming the same Animal must not dominate.
  • No stealing. Multiplayer competition is collection, Zoo Value, ranking, and shared Hunter scarcity — never attack, destruction, or theft.
  • No power inflation. New content is rarer, not stronger.
  • Historical / event identity is preserved. Old Animals do not become weaker than newer ones.
  • Monetisation is a future direction only. No prices, benefits, RevenueCat integration, or payment code in this ADR.
